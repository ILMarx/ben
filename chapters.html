Chapters

Borderland Education Network may develop part of its activity through chapters and other distributed units, whether physical or virtual, when these support its institutional aims. Each chapter is established with a defined remit, a designated coordinator, and an operational relationship with the central Board. Chapters do not function as separate legal entities, but as articulated branches of the same association.

Gorizia Chapter

15 April 2026 - 15 April 2027

Responsible: Prof. Rita Minello, Ph.D.

The Gorizia Chapter focuses on training for educators involved in social work with migrants, jointly with research on the same field of practice. Participating team members are listed under Training Fellowships.

Digital Chapter

Permanent

Responsible: Prof. Andrea Mattia Marcelli, Ph.D.

The Digital Chapter provides the permanent virtual environment required by the network’s activities. It ensures that Borderland Education Network operates not only through physical settings, but also through a stable digital infrastructure supporting the work of the other chapters.
