Mission

Borderland Education Network pursues scholarly, educational, and public-facing activities in the fields of community, education, migration, and social justice, with particular attention to pedagogical, social, and borderland contexts.

The network exists to promote research, training, cultural elaboration, and knowledge dissemination in areas concerning community life, educational processes, migration-related phenomena, inclusion, vulnerability, and social pedagogy.

Its activities include the development of scholarly inquiry, the organisation of training initiatives, the production of public-facing outputs, and the creation of spaces for critical reflection and interdisciplinary exchange.

Borderland Education Network encourages methodological rigour, intellectual freedom, ethical responsibility, and non-commercial knowledge circulation, including, where possible, the adoption of open and accessible dissemination practices.
