Borderland Education Network Library

The Library is the public-facing knowledge environment of Borderland Education Network. It brings together the network’s publishing activity and its preservation work within a shared non-commercial framework. It currently consists of two branches: BEN Press and Preservation Hub.

BEN Press

Original scholarly publishing within a full diamond open access and community-owned model.

Preservation Hub

A curated preservation space for openly licensed scholarly materials relevant to the network’s fields of interest.
